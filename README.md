# 🍳 X Fried Egg (xfriedegg)

A lightweight, vanilla-friendly Minecraft mod for **NeoForge** that turns simple eggs into a practical food source and valuable consumables across all stages of survival.

---

## ✨ Features

* **Fried Egg**
  * Smelt regular eggs in a **Furnace** or **Smoker**.
  * Restores **4 Nutrition** and **6 Saturation**.
  * Quick, accessible, and satisfying food source for early-game survival.
* **Golden Fried Egg**
  * Crafted using a Fried Egg surrounded by Gold Nuggets.
  * Restores **4 Nutrition** and **8 Saturation**.
  * Always edible, high-tier food option providing **Regeneration I** - perfect for mid-to-late game adventures, Nether raids, and boss fights.

---

## 💡 Why Fried Egg?

* **Balanced Progression:** Offers quick sustenance early on and scales into a valuable luxury food item later in the game.
* **Vanilla-Friendly:** Cleanly integrates into the game without unnecessary mechanics or clutter.

---

## 🛠️ Installation & Requirements

### Requirements
1. **Minecraft:** `26.3`
2. **Mod Loader:** **NeoForge** `26.3.0.95+`

### Installation
1. Download and install **NeoForge** (version `26.3.0.95` or higher) for Minecraft `26.3`.
2. Download the latest release of **X Fried Egg**.
3. Place the `.jar` file into your `.minecraft/mods` folder.
4. Launch the game and enjoy!
