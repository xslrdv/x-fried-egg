# 🍳 X Fried Egg

A vanilla-friendly **Minecraft** mod for **NeoForge** that turns simple eggs into a practical *food* source and valuable consumables across all stages of survival.

---

## ✨ Features

* **Fried Egg**
  * Cook regular eggs in a *Furnace*, *Smoker*, or on a *Campfire*.
  * Restores **4** *Nutrition* (2 Drumsticks) and **6** *Saturation*.
* **Golden Fried Egg**
  * Crafted using a **Fried Egg** surrounded by *Gold Nuggets*.
  * Restores **4** *Nutrition* and **8** *Saturation*.
  * Always edible (can be eaten even when full).
  * Grants *Regeneration I* (5 seconds).
* **Enchanted Golden Fried Egg**
  * Crafted using a **Golden Fried Egg** surrounded by **Lapis Lazuli Dust**.
  * Or crafted using a **Fried Egg** surrounded by *Gold Nuggets* and **Lapis Lazuli Dust**.
  * Restores **4** *Nutrition* and **10** *Saturation*.
  * Always edible.
  * Grants *Regeneration I* (5 seconds) and *Resistance I* (1 minute).
* **Lapis Lazuli Dust**
  * Obtained by crushing *Lapis Lazuli* using a *Pickaxe*.
  * Used as a crafting ingredient and for enchanting.

---

## 💡 Why Fried Egg?

* **Balanced Progression**: Offers quick sustenance early on and scales into a valuable luxury *food* item later in the game.
* **Vanilla-Friendly**: Cleanly integrates into the game without unnecessary mechanics or clutter.

---

## 🛠️ Installation & Requirements

### Requirements
1. **Minecraft**: `26.3`
2. Mod Loader: **NeoForge** `26.3.0.95+`

### Installation
1. Download and install **NeoForge** (version `26.3.0.95` or higher) for **Minecraft** `26.3`.
2. Download the latest release of **X Fried Egg**.
3. Place the `.jar` file into your `.minecraft/mods` folder.
4. Launch the game and enjoy!
